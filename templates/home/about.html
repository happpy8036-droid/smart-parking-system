ABOUT SMART PARKING

Smart and convenient parking management system.

Our Project

Smart Parking System is designed to make parking easier, faster and more convenient for users.

Users can search for parking locations, check available slots and book a parking slot online.

The system helps reduce parking time and provides a simple way to manage parking reservations.

Our Features

  • Search parking locations
  • View available parking slots
  • Online parking booking
  • Booking history
  • QR code for booking
  • Parking management
